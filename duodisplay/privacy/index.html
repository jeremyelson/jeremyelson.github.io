DuoDisplay

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

DuoDisplay doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no DuoDisplay servers. Your screen goes straight from your Mac to your iPhone over your own network.

What stays on your devices

  • Your Mac's screen. The Mac app captures only the extra display it creates, and only while a paired iPhone is connected. The picture is sent directly to that iPhone over your local network, encrypted with a key that only your paired devices know. It is never recorded or saved.
  • Touches and typing. Taps, scrolls and keystrokes on your iPhone are sent to your Mac as mouse and keyboard input over the same encrypted connection. They aren't logged.
  • Pairing keys. When you scan your Mac's pairing code, a secret key is saved in the Keychain on your iPhone and on your Mac. It never leaves those devices.
  • Free-time usage. To enforce the 5 free hours per week, the iPhone app counts how long you've streamed this week and keeps that count in its Keychain. It isn't sent anywhere.
  • Settings. Display preferences are saved on the device that uses them.

Permissions

  • Camera (iPhone), only to scan the pairing code on your Mac. No photos or video are kept.
  • Local Network (iPhone and Mac), to find and connect to your Mac on your Wi‑Fi.
  • Screen Recording and Accessibility (Mac), to show the extra display on your iPhone and to turn your touches into clicks and typing.

Purchases

DuoDisplay Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment and your subscription; DuoDisplay never sees your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

DuoDisplay doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
