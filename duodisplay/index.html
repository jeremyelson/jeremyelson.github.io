Your iPhone, as an extra display for your Mac.

A real extended display, over Wi‑Fi. Drag windows onto it, tap to click, scroll with two fingers. Built for iPhone Duo, and works on any iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

2 · Get the Mac app

A free menu bar app for macOS 14 Sonoma or later. It adds the extra display and sends it to your iPhone.

Download for Mac

Version 1.1 · 0.9 MB · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple silicon and Intel. Unzip it and drag DuoDisplay to your Applications folder.

3 · Pair once

Click the iPhone icon in your Mac's menu bar and scan the code with your iPhone. After that, just tap your Mac to connect.

Help

My Mac doesn't show up on my iPhone

Make sure both are on the same Wi‑Fi network and the Mac app is running. On your iPhone, check Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network and turn on DuoDisplay.

The display is black or won't connect

On your Mac, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and allow DuoDisplay under Screen Recording and Accessibility, then quit and reopen the Mac app.

“Pairing key mismatch”

The Mac's pairing was reset. Choose “Pair a Mac” on your iPhone and scan the new code.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel DuoDisplay Pro in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. To restore a purchase, open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases.
